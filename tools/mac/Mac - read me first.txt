BUNKER BUSTER for Mac
=====================

Works on Intel Macs (macOS 11 or newer) and Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 13 or newer).

The game isn't from the App Store and isn't signed with an Apple developer
certificate, so macOS asks you to approve it once. You approve a small
launcher, "Play Bunker Buster.command"; it then sets up the game for you.


FIRST TIME
----------
1. Unzip the download. You get a folder called "Bunker Buster".
   Put the whole folder wherever you like (Applications, Desktop, Documents).
   Keep everything inside it together.

2. Open the folder and double-click "Play Bunker Buster.command".
   macOS says it can't verify the file. Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
   Next to "Play Bunker Buster.command was blocked..." click "Open Anyway",
   then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   (On macOS 14 or older you can instead right-click the file, choose Open,
   then click Open.)

4. A Terminal window opens and the game starts. The first launch takes a few
   extra seconds while it prepares the game.
   If macOS asks whether Terminal may access a folder (Desktop, Downloads,
   Documents), click Allow.

Leave the Terminal window open while you play. Closing it quits the game.


AFTER THAT
----------
Just double-click "Play Bunker Buster.command".


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
The launcher writes a file called "bunker-buster-log.txt" in the Bunker Buster
folder. Send that file to David; it says what happened.
